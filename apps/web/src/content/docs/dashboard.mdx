# Dashboard

The Dashboard is your business command center. It shows real-time metrics that help you make decisions throughout the day.

## What You'll See

When you open the Dashboard, you'll see four key metrics cards at the top:

### Transaksi Hari Ini
**Total orders processed via POS today.**

This number includes all completed POS transactions for the current day. Use it to track daily sales volume.

### Pendapatan Hari Ini
**Gross revenue from sales today.**

This shows the total amount collected from all POS transactions. It's calculated before any deductions.

### Boarding Aktif
**Number of pets currently checked in.**

This includes all boardings with "Active" status across all branches. Draft and completed boardings are not counted.

### Low Stock
**Products with inventory at or below 5 units.**

Click this card to go directly to the Products page filtered by low stock items.

## Key Features

### Automatic Updates
The Dashboard updates automatically when:
- A new POS transaction is completed
- A boarding status changes (check-in or check-out)
- Product inventory changes

You don't need to refresh the page.

### Quick Navigation
Each metric card links to the relevant section:
- **Transaksi** → POS page
- **Pendapatan** → POS page
- **Boarding Aktif** → Boardings (filtered to Active status)
- **Low Stock** → Products (filtered to low stock)

### Occupancy Overview
The Occupancy card shows how full your branches are. It displays:
- Current active boardings
- Total capacity
- Available slots

## Getting the Most Out of Your Dashboard

### Morning Check
Start your day by checking the Dashboard. You'll see:
- How many pets are checked in
- Today's sales so far
- Which products need restocking

### Midday Review
Check again at midday to:
- Track if you're on pace for daily goals
- Identify slow-selling products
- Spot any operational issues

### End of Day
Review the final numbers to:
- Compare against previous days
- Identify trends
- Plan for tomorrow

## Best Practices

- **Check low stock daily** — Proactive restocking prevents lost sales
- **Monitor boarding capacity** — Accept new bookings before you're full
- **Track revenue patterns** — Identify your best days and times
